Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Who we are. MetriGlow Analytics (also called "MetriGlow", "we", "us") is an operating name used by an individual based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. It is not represented as a corporation or separate registered legal entity. For privacy inquiries, email metriglow@outlook.com.

This policy explains how we handle information in connection with our informational website, business inquiries, and analytics or reporting services. It describes our current approach and how additional integrations would operate when made available and authorized. We will update this policy if our practices change.

1. Information we collect

Website and inquiries: If you email us, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, business contact details and message. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not intentionally include a contact form, advertising pixels or custom analytics scripts. GitHub and your browser or network provider may process technical access information in accordance with their own practices.

Client reporting: When a business engages us, we may process the categories of business information it authorizes for its reporting requirements, including company identifiers, customers, vendors, accounting and sales transactions, invoice and payment records, line items, and related technical metadata. Those records can contain personal information about employees, customers or contacts.

Connection information: If a third-party integration is offered and authorized, we may process provider account identifiers, authorization status and access credentials or tokens necessary to maintain that connection. We do not ask clients for their QuickBooks passwords or multi-factor authentication codes.

2. Why and how we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, establish and manage authorized client connections, ingest and organize requested business records, create client reports, investigate data-quality issues, prevent unauthorized access, maintain systems, and fulfill legal or contractual obligations. We do not use client accounting records for targeted advertising or sell them to data brokers.

For any future production QuickBooks connection, an authorized business representative would grant permission through Intuit's authorization flow. The integration will not be treated as active until that production process is implemented and tested. Clients remain responsible for having the authority to authorize access to their company information.

3. Infrastructure, access and service providers

Our current technical solution uses Microsoft Azure components for data ingestion and storage, Azure SQL Database for reporting data, and Microsoft Power BI for reports. Its design separates client SQL schemas, Function Apps, managed identities and Power BI workspaces. Our present QuickBooks implementation uses development Sandbox data; production QuickBooks authorization is still being developed.

Information may be processed by vendors that support the service, including Microsoft for cloud services, GitHub for this website, Outlook for email, and Intuit where a QuickBooks connection is authorized. We disclose information to approved service providers as needed to provide the services or when required by law. We do not intentionally publish client financial records on this website.

4. Where data is processed

Core parts of our current Azure reporting setup are deployed in the Canada Central region. Not every component, provider log, support operation or third-party service is guaranteed to remain in Canada. Technical and business information may be processed or accessed in other jurisdictions as needed for the services, subject to applicable privacy obligations and service-provider terms.

5. Security and access controls

We work to limit access to authorized persons and systems and use client-specific configurations and permissions in our reporting architecture. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. We review suspected security incidents and make any required notifications under applicable law and client agreements. We do not describe our current integration as independently security-certified.

6. Retention, disconnection and deletion

We retain business information only for the purposes of providing the agreed services, troubleshooting, maintaining records required by law or resolving legitimate disputes. Retention depends on the type of data and the client engagement; we do not currently promise a fixed deletion period for every category. Raw files, reporting tables, operational logs and backups may have different retention lifecycles.

Authorized customers can request access, correction, export, disconnection or deletion by emailing us. We will verify the request, explain any applicable contractual or legal limits and confirm the available deletion or retention process. Disconnecting a third-party account does not automatically remove historical reporting data or existing backups. We will not represent automatic production-token revocation or deletion as implemented before it is tested.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, correct inaccuracies, withdraw consent where legally available, or raise a concern. We may need to verify your identity or authority before providing access to business or personal information. Some requests may need to be directed to the business customer that controls the relevant records.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this page to reflect new integrations, providers, security practices or legal requirements. The effective date above will change when a material revision is published. When legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain further authorization before using information for a new purpose.
