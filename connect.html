How an authorized connection works

1. Verify your business

Contact MetriGlow from your organization's approved business contact address. We confirm which QuickBooks company is being connected.

2. Follow your private invitation

MetriGlow will provide a short-lived individual authorization link when this service is ready. Sign in on Intuit's website and select your company.

3. Confirm completion

After authorization, MetriGlow verifies the company and confirms that your reporting connection is ready. Data synchronization is enabled only after setup and approval.
