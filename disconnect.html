QuickBooks · Disconnect guidance

Manage or end your connection.

You can withdraw an app's QuickBooks authorization in your company's QuickBooks integrations settings. Contact MetriGlow to confirm your service connection and stored reporting data are handled appropriately.

Disconnecting your QuickBooks company

Disconnect in QuickBooks

Use the Integrations area in QuickBooks and select the app's Disconnect option. A company administrator may be required.

Let MetriGlow know

Ask us to confirm that ongoing access or scheduled synchronization is disabled and to review any retained records under your client agreement.

Review stored data

Disconnecting access does not automatically remove historical reports, files, logs, or backups. You may request a separate review or deletion.
